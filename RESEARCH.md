# Research note (literature pointers)

**Not simulated in this repo.** The code is classical Kuramoto throughout. This file is a short, ordered list of topics that sometimes appear next to "microtubules" in the literature. None of them is implemented, measured, or claimed here. Status labels are about the literature, not about this package.

1. **Objective-reduction proposals (e.g. Orch OR).** Speculative. No consensus measurement of the required coherence in living neurons. Not simulated here.
2. **Warm-biology optical / collective-oscillation reports.** Optical or computational signatures discussed in recent papers are not the dynamics of this classical ring. Not simulated here.
3. **Stellar nucleosynthesis.** Carbon chemistry is forged in stars. True of ordinary matter generally; not informative for this model.
4. **Asteroseismology and protein normal modes.** Shared mathematics of coupled oscillators across scales. That shared formalism is the only link this simulation uses.
5. **Cross-frequency coupling (neuroscience).** Established motif: slow rhythms can modulate fast local computation (e.g. theta–gamma phase–amplitude coupling). The simulation extends that motif as a *model* of unstructured gain modulation; it does not use neural or stellar recordings.

**What this simulation found (classical only):** unstructured slow gain modulation degraded holographic recall monotonically, with no 1:1 phase locking across scales and no resonance peak in the original frequency scan. Reading: structured, gated coupling is a different mechanism from raw slow drive.

No claim of cosmic influence on brains is made or supported.
